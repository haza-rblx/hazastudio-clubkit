## Explored and kept (owner-approved along the way)

- **Fit to screen:** a UIScale on `9-AdvancedDJ`. The panel is a fixed 1250 px wide and was clipped on smaller screens.
- **Header:**
  - `DJ BOOTH` + a red `ON AIR` pill (pulsing, excluded from accent tint) + "24 listening".
  - A crowd-hype bar.
  - `DROP` (accent colour): a 3-2-1 overlay, a flash, then "DROP!".
  - `TAKE OVER`, minimise and close, not wired yet.
  - The whole header sits on one 52 px grid.
- **Effect pads:** a 3 px category strip on the left (orange = SFX, blue = WORLD). The cooldown shows in the label as "Air Horn · 3s"; 3 s for SFX, 8 s for WORLD.
- **Library:** `Library / Requests 3 / Queue 3` tabs, aligned with the search box.
  - Requests rows show avatar, track, "Requested by … · N votes" and **Play next / Skip**.
  - Queue rows show BPM and **Load B**.
  - Lists leave room for the floating "Add from ID" dock.
- **Centre column (30% wide):**
  - Two vertical scrolling waveform lanes (A blue, B orange).
  - A playhead at 35%, with the played part dimmed, and a beat grid.
  - BPM tags at the top, a 4-beat phase meter at the bottom.
  - Per-deck VU in its own gutter, driven by the real music `PlaybackLoudness`.
- **Deck geometry:** the deck row was 237 px and is now 301. Per deck:
  - SYNC/speed column, 20%.
  - Platter, plus a HIGH/MID/LOW row under it, 63%. The platter is 181 px.
  - FILTER knob over a long volume fader, 17%.
  - All columns share 14 px top/bottom padding, so tops and bottoms line up. This was measured.
- **Fader caps:** matte grey, white centre indicator, faint grip grooves.
- **Knobs** (all 43 px): dark disc, white pointer, 11-dot LED ring.
  - The dots sit on rotated arms (sub-pixel), so the spacing is even. Pixel-snapped offsets looked uneven.
  - The dots don't rotate; they light up to the value.
  - **FILTER is bipolar:** OFF in the middle; drag down for LPF (blue), up for HPF (orange). It has a centre detent, and a double-click resets it to OFF.
- **Jog wheel:** the owner's original look is kept (they rejected a matte restyle). Dragging around the ring scratches, turning the marker.

## 2026-10-10: djay Pro as the visual target

The owner named djay Pro (Algoriddim) as the UI language. The layout already matched it (track strip, decks around a centre waveform, transport, mixer row, pads, library), so the work is the visual language, in this order:

1. **Surface (done, section 10 of the script).** Neutral greys (`C.gutter` 0a0a0a, tiles 1c1c1c, raised 2a2a2a, inset 141414). Corners are 4 px on buttons, 0 on pads and 6 on the window. No borders.
   - **One line weight: 2 px gutters everywhere**, including the pads and the hot-cue segment dividers.
   - **Every tile is sized in whole pixels** on a column grid:

     | Row | Widths (px) |
     |---|---|
     | Main row | 436, 374, 436 |
     | Mixer row | 217, 217, 374, 217, 217 |
     | Pads | 4 × 311 |
     | Library | 311, 937 |
     | Transport and mixer row height | 56 |

     The panel height must stay even (908).
   - The owner flagged the first pass as "ada yang tebel ada yang tipis ada yang double". The cause was Scale widths: 0.35 × 1250 = 437.5 put edges on half pixels, so the same 2 px gutter rendered 1–3 px. The hot cues also had a 1.5 px rim, and the white CanvasGroup backing showed between the buttons.
   - `StarterGui.DJGUI` now carries `AccentNoTint` (set in Edit). The accent tint decides exclusion only when it first sees an instance, so setting it from the LocalScript is too late.
   - With the tint off, the owner's own design colours show again: the red jog marker and the magenta cover placeholder.
2. Pads: flat 8×2 grid.
3. FX row: 3 slots per deck (`‹ Echo ▾ ›`, ON, slider, D/W).
4. Library: icon source column, text tabs (active orange), right-aligned BPM/key/length, playing row highlighted.
5. Track strip: overview waveform under the title.
6. Transport: segmented buttons.

**Waveform stays vertical** (owner, 2026-10-10). Only its colours and content move toward djay.
- **Done (section 6), live in the playtest.** Spectral rows: kick = red, off-beat hats = blue, the rest drifts through magenta, and the noise makes the edges spiky. Grey beat lines, pale-yellow bar lines with bar numbers that count up as the lane scrolls, a red playhead. No dimming of the played part.

  - fill `1b1b1b`, rim `060606` at 1.5 px;

  Two changes only. The corners went from 8 to 4 ("agak kotakin"), and the sizes are whole pixels (cue buttons 60 px each). The picker card is hidden in the live session; it is not in the script.

- **Knobs: back to the LED-dot ring (owner, 2026-10-10).** I tried the djay arc knob in three passes:
  - a flat disc with a thin arc;
  - a raised gradient cap with a channel;
  - a flat face with the arc painted on the rim.

  The owner also saw 20 tweaks of that knob, then 20 truly different concepts (segment ring, Ableton faceless, donut, orbit dot, gauge, TE squircle, solid cap, wireframe, bezel ticks, notch, tinted face, halo, bars, number chip, tick ladder, stepped pills, concentric, knurled, − 0 + scale). ## 2026-10-11: the panel plays real audio (section 13 of DJXExperiment)

Place-only mockup, client-local (nothing goes to the server). Source: `docs/plans/dj-panel-experiment/DJXExperiment.client.luau`, section 13 (uncommitted).

- **Library -> decks.** Library rows come from `MusicPlayerStore`. The store is empty until the real music panel's Request tab has loaded; fetch `MusicGetPlaylists` / `MusicGetTracks` and `store:set` to fill it by hand. Drag a row onto Deck A/B (tile or track tile): ghost card, green 3 px `DropOutline`, drop loads the deck. The drop is decided on the release point, not on hover.
- **Audio.** One `Sound` per deck, parts chained, `PlaybackSpeed = track.playbackSpeed x deck speed readout`. Real length comes from `Sound.TimeLength` (stored `partDurations` disagree with the asset).
- **Crossfader.** Reads `DraggableHandle.Position.X.Scale / span`. Gain chases the target (tau 50 ms), 3 % dead zone at each end, 3 % magnet at 50 %, curve pill SMOOTH / CUT / LINEAR (the pill sits under the handle). The handle is left to `AdvancedDJController`.
- **EQ.** Knob `Rotation` (-135 + v * 270) -> dB: 0 dB centre, +6 dB max, -12 dB at 0.25, kill at 0. One `EqualizerSoundEffect` per deck, read-out rewritten in dB.
- **FILTER.** Second `EqualizerSoundEffect` in series (LPF cuts High, HPF cuts Low, Mid from 60 %). Coarse: bands have engine-fixed edges.
- **Jog wheel.** Read-out: remaining %, deck speed (track tempo counts as 1.00X), elapsed, remaining. The red platter turns 180 deg/s x speed, written as an absolute angle each frame (the controller adds its own fixed spin). Scratch deltas go through `jogScratch`.
- **Hot cues 1-5.** Tap empty = store, tap lit = jump, right-click = clear (clear NOT verified: the input simulator may not send right-clicks). Kept per track for the session. Colours red / orange / lime / blue / purple.
- **Waveform strip** in the track tile (replaces the 2 px progress line): 250 bars with vertical UIGradients, played part dimmed, red playhead, cue arrows. DECORATIVE: Roblox cannot read samples, so the shape is a Random seeded by the track id (quiet intro, loud blocks, breaks). Owner chose this over live-recorded or VPS-analysed data. Known: one stretch after the intro renders pale grey, unlike the djay reference. For real shapes the data must come from the VPS.
- **Not done:** loop / beat jump (needs a BPM per track; the 124.0 / 122.0 in the header are mock); auto-crossfade; the drag card and hover outline were never seen working with a real cursor (the simulator cannot move the mouse).

**Testing traps.** `simulate_mouse_input` cannot move the cursor and its button state desyncs ("duplicate button state"); its coordinates follow the screenshot size, which changes when the panel refits. `loadstring` is off in the client: patch by recreating a LocalScript. `Enum.Font.Inter` does not exist (it silently killed the drag card): use `Font.new("rbxassetid://12187365364")`. A `BindableEvent` `DJXLoadDeck` (`Fire("A", trackId)`) loads a deck without dragging.
